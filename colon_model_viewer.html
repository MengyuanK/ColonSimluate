Fitted to Alazmani et al. 2016 (24 healthy colons, CT colonography, supine). Each segment matches the paper's mean length and mean bend angle. The 3D layout is schematic, not one patient's scan.

Capsule run

Fit check: a rigid capsule crossing a bend of radius R needs about d + R − √(R² − (L/2)²) of lumen. It uses the diameter set chosen above. The inflated set is optimistic for a capsule, which usually meets a collapsed colon. Alazmani's design limit for a rigid device is 17 mm wide, the 5th-percentile sigmoid diameter.

Turning points

Bend angle uses Alazmani's method: the direction change between 35 mm before and after each point on a smoothed centreline (0° = straight). Flexure values are means over the 10 cm around the flexure, as in the paper's Table 4.

Sources and assumptions

  1. Lengths (Table 1), diameters (Table 2), mean bend angles (Table 4) and landmark definitions: Alazmani A, Hood A, Jayne D, Neville A, Culmer P. Quantitative assessment of colorectal morphology: implications for robotic colonoscopy. Med Eng Phys 2016;38:148–154. Supine means.
  2. Uninflated diameters (optional set): abdominal CT without insufflation, J Clin Med 2023;12:341.
  3. Not from the paper: the 3D layout. A textbook frame path was placed by hand, then smooth 3D wiggles were added. Their size, the transverse hang and the sigmoid apex height were solved so that each segment's length and mean bend angle match the paper.
  4. Real colons vary widely: length SDs are about 15–30% per segment, and bend-angle SDs about ±20°. Treat this as an average colon, not a worst case.
